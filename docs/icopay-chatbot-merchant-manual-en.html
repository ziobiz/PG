1. What is Chatbot Payment?

ICOPAY Chatbot Payment is a service where an AI chatbot guides customers through product introduction → order collection → payment in a single conversation flow. Without building a separate shopping site or app, merchants can run online sales, reservations, and payments with just one chatbot URL.

Customer Payment Flow (e.g. Prepaid Sale)

Customer→Accesses the chatbot URL distributed by the merchant (link click or QR scan)
AI Chatbot→Introduces products registered by the merchant and prompts selection
Customer→Selects product and fills in order information
AI Chatbot→Guides to payment page
Customer→Completes card payment
Merchant→Views order in Order Management screen

What Merchants Operate vs. What HQ Manages

Merchant Can Do DirectlyHQ (Headquarters) Manages
Chatbot operation mode, vertical, reservation settingsChatbot payment activation / deactivation
Product add, edit, deleteProduct registration limit
View order listApproval email / LINE Notify alert setup
View, copy chatbot URL / QR / embed scriptCompany basic information changes

2. Before You Start (HQ Activation Required)

Chatbot Payment must be activated by HQ on your merchant account before use. Check the following:

Check ItemHow to Verify
Is Chatbot Management visible in the sidebar?If visible, activation is complete. If not, contact HQ.
Is a Chatbot Payment URL shown in My Company Info?If a URL is displayed, the account is active.
Items that require HQ request
The following cannot be changed by merchants directly — contact your ICOPAY HQ representative:

• Chatbot payment activation / deactivation
• Payment approval email notification setup
• LINE Notify alert setup
• Product registration limit increase
• Company basic info changes (name, address, etc.)

3. STEP 1 Viewing Your Chatbot Payment Info (View Only)

Company Management > My Company Info
This screen is view-only. Merchants can view and copy information on this page but cannot directly modify any settings.

3-1. View and Copy Your Chatbot Payment URL

Once HQ activates chatbot payment, the following URL will be displayed automatically:

https://pay.icopay.co.kr/chatbot-pay/{merchant-code}
ActionHow
Copy URLClick the Copy button next to the URL field → saved to clipboard
Download QR CodeClick the Save button next to the QR image → downloads as PNG file
If Chatbot Payment is set to disabled by HQ, the URL will not appear. Please contact your HQ representative to request activation.

3-2. Copy the Website Floating Widget Script

To display a chatbot icon on your website, copy the Chatbot Floating Widget code from My Company Info:

<!-- Example of copied code — paste before </body> on all pages -->
<script src="https://pay.icopay.co.kr/v1/embed-chatbot/{merchant-code}"
        async defer charset="utf-8"></script>
On the My Company Info screen, press the Copy button to instantly copy the complete code including your merchant code.

3-3. Using the QR Code

  • Send the downloaded PNG to a print shop or use it in poster and business card design
  • Place on table cards, signboards, and menus inside your store
  • Customers scan QR → go directly to chatbot payment page

4. STEP 2 Chatbot Basic Settings ✔ Merchant Operated

Chatbot Management > Basic Settings
Merchants can configure and save these settings directly.

4-1. Select Operation Mode

Choose the mode that matches your business type and payment timing. The AI will guide customers through the appropriate conversation flow.

ModeDescriptionSuitable Industries
Product Sale · PrepaidImmediate payment after product selectionOnline shop, merchandise sales
Product Sale · PostpaidOrder received, payment collected laterCustom orders, B2B supply
Reservation · PrepaidImmediate payment upon bookingMassage, hair salon, study room
Reservation · PostpaidReservation accepted → payment on visitRestaurant, consulting
Hybrid (Sale + Reservation)Mix of regular sale and reservation productsMulti-service businesses
Face-to-Face (Sale+Reservation · Postpaid)Confirm on-site before paymentVIP club, in-person services

4-2. Select Business Vertical

Choose the business type closest to yours so the AI chatbot automatically constructs the right question flow for customers.

VerticalAI Response Characteristics
General Sale / E-CommerceCollects product name, quantity, shipping address
Massage (General / Club)Collects reservation date, time, course, guest count
RestaurantCollects guest count, date, time, special requests
Consulting / ServiceCollects topic, preferred schedule, contact info
Real Estate / Auto SalesCollects property interest, budget, available visit dates
VIP ClubMembership and reservation course-focused guidance
Business Vertical Notes (Optional): Enter any specific info that must be collected or expressions to avoid. This is reflected in AI responses.

4-3. Basic Business Information

Information the AI references when answering customer inquiries. If left blank, it defaults to the registered company information.

  • Business name / Address / Phone number
  • Business hours
  • Additional notes (parking, transit directions, etc.)

4-4. Reservation Settings (for Reservation Mode)

SettingDescription
Default Reservation Slot (min)Booking time unit: 15 / 30 / 45 / 60 / 90 / 120 / 180 / 240 minutes
Individual products can override this in Product Management
Reservation Timezone (IANA)Reference timezone (default: Asia/Seoul)
If a time slot already has a booking, the AI automatically suggests alternative times. Duplicate bookings are blocked automatically by slot unit.

8. Operation Mode Flow Summary

Prepaid Mode — Immediate Payment

Customer→Accesses chatbot URL
AI→Introduces products registered by the merchant
Customer→Selects product + fills in order information
AI→Guides to payment page → card payment completed
Merchant→Views order in Order Management (email/LINE alert if configured by HQ)

Postpaid Mode — Order Received, Pay Later

Customer→Accesses chatbot URL → selects product and submits order info
Merchant→Checks order in Order Management → contacts customer
Payment→Processed separately (on-site, bank transfer, etc.)

Reservation Mode — Select Date & Time

Customer→Accesses chatbot URL
AI→Shows available dates and times (based on slot setting in Basic Settings)
Customer→Selects date, time, guest count + fills in personal info
Prepaid→Immediate payment → reservation confirmed
Postpaid→Reservation accepted → payment on visit
Merchant→Views reservation date/time and customer info in Order Management

9. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

The Chatbot Management menu is not visible in the sidebar.
HQ has not yet activated chatbot payment for your merchant account. Contact your ICOPAY HQ representative to request activation. Merchants cannot change this setting directly.
No Chatbot Payment URL is shown in My Company Info.
This means chatbot payment is currently set to disabled by HQ. The URL only appears after HQ activates it. Please contact your HQ representative.
I want to edit my company information.
My Company Info is view-only for merchants. If you need to update any information, please contact your ICOPAY HQ representative.
I want to receive email or LINE notifications when payments are approved.
Notification settings cannot be changed by merchants directly. Contact your HQ representative and provide your email address or LINE Notify token to have it configured.
I registered a product but it's not showing in the chatbot.
Check the product's Active Status in Product Management. Inactive products are not shown to customers. Also check that the current date falls within any configured sale period.
Can overlapping reservations be made?
If a time slot already has a booking, the AI automatically suggests alternative times. Duplicate bookings are blocked automatically by the slot unit configured in Basic Settings.
I can no longer register new products.
You have reached the product registration limit set by HQ. Delete unnecessary products or contact HQ to request a limit increase.
I want to edit or delete an order.
Order Management is view-only for merchants. If order changes are needed, please contact your ICOPAY HQ representative.
Can the chatbot respond to customers in multiple languages?
ICOPAY Chatbot automatically responds in the customer's browser language. Multilingual support (Korean, English, Japanese, Chinese, Thai, etc.) is available without any additional configuration.
