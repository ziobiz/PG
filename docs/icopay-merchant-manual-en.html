3-2. Checkout API (ICOPAY unified)

Use the endpoints and tables shown on the Merchant API screen. Main paths:

API TypeEndpointDescription

One-time Payment (Session)/api/middleware/v1/merchant/checkout/sessionSession lookup

3-3. Integration steps

①Request HQ API deploy and broker secret issuance
②Open Merchant API — confirm keys, quick start, parameter tables
③Call Prepare on the merchant server; pass only sessionToken/embed to the browser
④Confirm PAID via Status API or Webhook on the server

4-3. How to Request a Refund

Payment Management > Payment History > Detail > Refund Request
①Select the transaction to refund → Click View Detail
②Click the Refund Request button
③Enter refund amount (full or partial refund)
④Enter refund reason
⑤Click Submit Refund to proceed
⑥Status updates to Refund Completed when processed
Refund Eligibility:
  • Only successful transactions can be refunded
  • Refund must be requested within a certain period (typically 90 days) after payment
  • Already refunded amounts cannot be refunded again
  • Partial refund cannot exceed the original payment amount

11. FAQ — Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a refund take to reach the customer?
After a refund request is submitted, processing typically takes 3–7 business days. The actual timeline depends on the payment method (credit card, wallet, QR, etc.) and the original payment date. Track refund status under Payment History > Refund tab.
I lost or accidentally exposed my API broker secret. Can I reissue it?
Broker secret reissue is HQ-only. Ask ICOPAY HQ if exposure is suspected. The new secret invalidates the old one immediately — update your server config at once. Specs and samples remain on the Merchant API screen after login.
A customer wants to cancel their subscription. Do I need to refund the current billing period?
Whether to refund the current billing period depends on your service policy. Cancel the subscription in Subscription Payment History, then if needed, submit a partial or full refund for that billing period separately from the Payment History screen.
When is my settlement paid? Can I see the upcoming settlement schedule?
Settlement frequency and payout dates are determined by your ICOPAY contract terms. You can check the next settlement date in Settlement > Merchant Settlement History. Contact your ICOPAY account manager to change the settlement schedule.
I forgot my password. How can I reset it?
Click Forgot Password on the ICOPAY login page to receive a reset link via email. If you cannot access your registered email, contact ICOPAY customer support at https://icopay.co.kr for identity verification and password reset assistance.
Where do I configure the payment notification (Webhook/Notify) URL?
Notification URL settings are managed by the ICOPAY operations team and cannot be changed directly from the merchant portal. Submit a notification URL registration or change request to your ICOPAY account manager. For real-time payment status verification, use the status API: /api/middleware/v1/merchant/checkout/status.
I cannot add more chatbot products. I see a "slot limit reached" message.
You have reached your contracted product slot limit. Deactivate or delete unused products to free up slots, or contact your ICOPAY account manager to increase the slot limit.
Can I download payment records to Excel?
Yes. Payment History, URL Payment History, Subscription History, and Settlement History screens all have a CSV Export or Excel Download button. Apply your search filters first to download only the data you need.
A customer missed an installment payment in a split contract. What should I do?
Check the overdue installment in Split Payment > Progress Management. Copy the installment payment URL and resend it to the customer. If the due date has passed, check Email Management for resend options or contact your ICOPAY account manager for overdue handling procedures.
