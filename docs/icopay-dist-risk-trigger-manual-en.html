1. What this guide covers

HQ sets platform-wide safeguards under Risk Settings and Risk Filtering. If a merchant uses Follow HQ policy, the defaults below apply as-is.

AreaIn shortOutcome
Risk SettingsNon-success attempts accumulate on the same cardCooldown wait → inactive card after N failures
Risk FilteringFormat / velocity / contact issues (before sending to PG)No PG call · cancel on ledger · Risk dashboard

One failure does not permanently ban a card. Filtering blocks that attempt only; Risk Settings escalate by count and wait time.

2. When defaults apply

  • Merchant risk policy = Follow HQ (or unset → follow HQ)
  • HQ has not changed system defaults (figures in this doc = built-in defaults)
With Custom merchant policy, only tier waits and auto-inactive tier override HQ. Presale filter switches and velocity windows/attempts remain HQ-wide.

3. Risk Settings — defaults & triggers

Non-success (FAIL, cancel, void, unpaid, etc.) on the same card adds a wait before the next try. CVV/format errors are excluded; a successful payment resets the count.

1 non-success → 5 min cooldown 2 → 10 min 3 → 1 hour 4 → inactive card (long block until manual release) 1 success → reset count
JPAY postsale high-risk / PY0124 (when HQ switches are On) also count as one failure toward this cooldown. If Off, they may still log to the dashboard but do not count.

7. FAQ

Q. Does one failure permanently ban the card?
A. No. Defaults are cooldowns (5→10→60 min); at 4 failures the card is auto-registered inactive. Success resets the count.
Q. What do I see when filtering blocks?
A. No transaction at the processor; ledger shows cancel; Risk dashboard lists the event.
Q. Can distributors change 10 min / 3 card velocity?
A. No — only HQ Risk Filtering.
Q. If merchant risk is Off?
A. Only that merchant’s card cooldown / auto-inactive stops. HQ presale filtering still runs.
