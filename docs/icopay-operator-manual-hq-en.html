🔒 HQ / Distributor Access Restriction Notice

HQ and Distributor operators cannot access the menus listed below. If those features are needed, please contact the Super Admin (Head Office).

  • HQ Policy (All 8 hub groups) — Fee & Risk, Payment & URL, Notification Center, Settlement & Business Days, Organization & Screens, Access & Permissions, AI & Chatbot, Platform
  • Integration & Deploy — PG Integration, Merchant API Release are restricted (API Docs tab is readable)
  • Settlement Management — Integrated Settlement, Settlement Execution, Settlement Deploy, Settlement Pending are restricted
  • Notification Management — Entirely restricted (including Payment Notify URL and alert settings)
  • User Management — Menu Item Order Management is restricted
  • Operations Management — Main Operations, TAX Report, Verification Report, Agency Transaction History are restricted
  • Split Payment Management — Email Settings is restricted

2. Merchant Management

Merchant Management

Handles merchant registration, updates, fee management, and change history. Both HQ and Distributor have full access.

2-1. New Merchant Registration

Merchant Management > Merchant Management
①Go to Merchant Management > Merchant Management → Click [New Registration]
②Enter basic information — Business name, registration number, representative name, contact, email, address
③Configure settlement structure — Settlement cycle, fee rate, bank account
④Activate payment services (Web Payment, Subscription, Repurchase, Split, Chatbot)
⑤Save → Merchant code auto-generated → Check payment URL
⑥Create merchant account (ID & temporary password) and notify the contact person

2-2. Merchant Info Lookup & Edit

Merchant Management > Merchant Info Lookup
  • Search by merchant name, business registration number, or status (active/inactive)
  • View merchant details → Edit Basic Info / Payment Settings / Settlement Settings tabs
  • Switch merchant status: Active / Suspended / Inactive
  • Announcements tab: Create and manage notices for merchants

2-3. Fee Management

Merchant Management > Fee Management

Set individual fee rates per merchant, applied independently of the base HQ Policy fees (Super Admin only).

①Select the target merchant
②Enter individual fee rates per payment method (credit card, wallet transfer, QR, etc.)
③Set application period (start/end date) — supports promotional fee periods
④Save → Applied immediately to that merchant only (overrides base policy)
When individual fee rates are set for a merchant, they take priority over the base HQ Policy fee. Merchants without individual rates automatically use the base policy.
🔒 Base Fee Policy (HQ Policy > Fee & Risk) is Super Admin only.
Contact the Super Admin if changes to the base fee policy are required.

2-4. Merchant Change History

Merchant Management > Merchant Change History
  • View all merchant data change history (change time, field, before/after values)
  • Filter by operator
  • Export history as CSV

3. Payment Management

Payment Management

View payment history and process refunds and cancellations per merchant.

3-1. Payment History

Payment Management > Payment History
Search CriteriaDescription
PeriodPayment date range (up to 90 days)
MerchantFilter by merchant name or merchant code
Payment MethodCredit card, wallet transfer, QR, virtual account, etc.
Payment StatusSuccess / Failed / Cancelled / Refunded / Pending
Order NumberDirect search by merchant order ID

3-2. Refund Processing

Payment Management > Payment History > Detail
①Select the transaction to be refunded from Payment History
②In transaction detail → Click [Request Refund]
③Enter refund amount (full or partial)
④Enter refund reason and confirm
⑤After refund completion, status changes to Refunded
Note: Refunds are only available within 180 days of the original payment. For transactions beyond this period, contact the Super Admin.

3-3. Cancellation Processing

Payment Management > Payment History > Detail
  • Same-day transactions: Immediate void processing available
  • Transactions after the next day: Processed as refund
  • Automatic notification sent to merchant and buyer after cancellation

3-4. Integrated History / Daily Integrated / Integrated Check

Payment Management > Integrated History / Daily Integrated / Integrated Check
  • Integrated History: Unified transaction list across all PG channels
  • Daily Integrated: Daily aggregated statistics — transaction count and total amount
  • Integrated Check: Verify data consistency between PG notification data and internal records

5. Chatbot Management

Chatbot Management
✅ Chatbot Management (Basic Settings, Product Management, Order Management) is fully accessible by both HQ and Distributor.

5-1. Basic Settings

Chatbot Management > Basic Settings
①Select merchant → Choose chatbot operation mode
②Set product slot count (maximum number of products merchant can register)
③Confirm chatbot payment URL and QR code, then notify merchant
④Issue embed script — for widget insertion on merchant's website
Operation ModeDescriptionPayment Type
SALE_PREPAIDSale — PrepaidImmediate payment at order
SALE_POSTPAIDSale — PostpaidInvoice after product delivery
RESERVATION_PREPAIDReservation — PrepaidImmediate payment at reservation
RESERVATION_POSTPAIDReservation — PostpaidOn-site payment after reservation
HYBRID_RESERVATION_PREPAIDHybrid Reservation — PrepaidDeposit upfront + balance later
FACE_TO_FACE_POSTPAIDFace-to-Face — PostpaidPayment link sent after on-site service

5-2. Product Management

Chatbot Management > Product Management
①Select merchant → Access product list
②Register product: name, description, price, image, category, stock availability
③Activate/deactivate products (controls visibility in chatbot)
④If slot limit is reached, no more products can be added → adjust slot count

5-3. Order Management

Chatbot Management > Order Management
  • View order number, customer info, payment status, amount, and date/time
  • View order details and process refunds
  • Postpaid orders: Re-send payment request link
  • Order statistics: Daily/weekly/monthly order trends

6. Split Payment Management

②Set total payment amount and number of installments (e.g., ฿10,000 / 3 installments → ฿3,333 × 3)
③Set due date for each installment
④Save contract → Payment URL auto-generated per installment
⑤Send installment payment URL to customer (email, SMS, or link share)

9. FAQ — Frequently Asked Questions

The HQ Policy menu is not visible.
In ICOPAY V2.0, the former "HQ Settings" was renamed to "HQ Policy" hub group and is Super Admin only. See HQ Policy > Platform > Release notes for version history. Contact Super Admin for fee, risk, or payment URL policy changes.
I cannot access the Notification Management menu.
Notification Management is a Super Admin-only menu. For changes to payment notify URLs or alert settings, contact the Super Admin. HQ/Distributor can view notification receive history in Notification Management under Operations Management.
The Settlement Execution and Deploy menus are not visible.
Settlement Execution, Deploy, Pending, and Integrated Settlement are Super Admin-only menus. Merchant settlement history, fee records, and balance status are viewable in the Settlement Management sub-menus. Contact the Super Admin for settlement processing.
The split payment Email Settings is disabled.
Split Payment Management > Email Settings is Super Admin only. Contact the Super Admin for email template changes. Email send history and re-sending are available in Split Payment Management > Email Management.
A merchant says they cannot see the payment URL after I activated the service.
Please check that the relevant service's usage status is set to Y in Merchant Info. If it is N, the URL will not be displayed in the merchant portal. After saving, advise the merchant to re-login or refresh the page.
Where can I find the API documentation?
Go to Integration & Deploy > Merchant API Release > API Docs tab. HQ and Distributor roles can read this tab. PG integration execution is Super Admin only.
What is the difference between Fee Management and HQ Policy fee settings?
Merchant Management > Fee Management sets individual fee rates applied only to specific merchants. HQ Policy > Fee & Risk is the system-wide base fee policy, accessible only by the Super Admin. Merchants with individual fee rates will use those rates in priority over the base policy.
Is there a difference in access rights between HQ and Distributor?
No, HQ and Distributor have exactly the same access rights. Both roles cannot access Super Admin-only menus (HQ Policy, Notification Management, Settlement Execution/Deploy/Pending, etc.).
